Grammar rule: "Ich möchte..." + infinitive at the end, everywhere

Notice the same modal-verb sentence bracket from Chapter 16 running through every single phrase in this chapter — from the supermarket to the bank, möchte takes position 2, and the real action verb waits until the very end: Ich möchte ein Konto eröffnen. This is why Chapter 16 was worth learning solidly — it pays off constantly.

Common mistake: forgetting "das gefällt mir" flips subject and object

Reusing Chapter 19's rule: to say you like an item of clothing, the item is the subject and you are in the dative — Das gefällt mir, not "Ich gefalle das."

Übungen (Exercises)

Try it yourself

1. Ergänzen Sie: Ich ___ (möchten) einen Pullover ___ (kaufen).
2. Wie fragt man auf Deutsch: "Where is the changing room?"
3. Übersetzen Sie: "I like this jacket, but it's too small."
4. Sie sind in der Bank. Wie sagen Sie: "I would like to withdraw 100 euros"?
5. Write a short shopping dialogue (4–5 lines) at the Kaufhaus, including asking for help, trying something on, and mentioning size or price.
