Daily routine — putting separable verbs and time to work together

Original topic sheet View "Lesetext – Mein Alltag" in the Picture Book →

This chapter is mostly application — combining telling time (Chapter 13) with separable verbs (Chapter 21) to describe a normal day. Along the way, a few new daily-routine verbs.

💬 Example: a short daily-routine text

Ich stehe um sieben Uhr auf. (I get up at seven.)

Ich dusche und frühstücke um halb acht. (I shower and have breakfast at half past seven.)

Um neun Uhr gehe ich zur Arbeit. (At nine o'clock I go to work.)

Am Abend komme ich um sechs Uhr nach Hause und sehe fern. (In the evening I come home at six and watch TV.)

Ich gehe um elf Uhr ins Bett. (I go to bed at eleven.)

📐 Grammar rule: time expressions often go to position 1

When you start a sentence with a time word (um neun Uhr, am Abend, morgens...), remember Chapter 5's verb-second rule: the verb still comes second, and the subject moves right after it — Um neun Uhr gehe ich..., not "Um neun Uhr ich gehe..."

Exercises

✏️ Try it yourself

1. Write three sentences describing your own morning routine, using at least two separable verbs.
2. Translate: "First I get up, then I shower."
3. Fix the word order: "Um acht Uhr ich frühstücke."
4. Translate: "In the evening I watch TV and go to bed at ten."
