Gefallen und Missfallen ausdrücken

Expressing likes and dislikes

Original topic sheet View "Gefallen und Missfallen ausdrücken" in the Picture Book →

German has three common ways to say you like something, and they don't use grammar interchangeably — this chapter is really about untangling that, building directly on Chapter 19's dative pronouns.

Grammar rule: gefallen (dative + nominative) vs. mögen/finden (nominative + accusative)

After gefallen, the thing liked stays in the nominative and the person doing the liking is in the dative — you met this exact structure in Chapter 19. After mögen and finden, the sentence works like a normal transitive verb: the person is the subject (nominative), and the thing liked is the accusative object (Chapter 14).

VerbPatternExample
gefallen[thing, nominative] + gefällt + [person, dative]Der Film gefällt mir. (I like the movie.)
mögen[person, nominative] + mag + [thing, accusative]Ich mag den Film. (I like the movie.)

Three ways to say you like something

GermanEnglish
Mir gefällt...I like... (literally: ... pleases me)
Ich mag...I like...
Ich finde ... gut/toll/super.I find ... good/great/super.
Ich liebe...I love...

And for dislikes

GermanEnglish
Mir gefällt ... nicht.I don't like...
Ich mag ... nicht.I don't like...
Ich finde ... nicht gut/doof/langweilig.I find ... not good/silly/boring.
Ich hasse...I hate...

Mini-Dialog

A: Wie findest du den Film?
A: What do you think of the movie?

B: Ich finde ihn toll! Und die Musik?
B: I find it great! And the music?

A: Die finde ich nicht gut.
A: I don't find that good.

Common mistake: "Ich mag der Film" instead of "den Film"

Because mögen "feels" similar to gefallen in meaning, learners often keep the noun in the nominative after it too. But mögen takes a direct object, so it must be accusative: Ich mag den Film (masculine der → den), not "Ich mag der Film."

Übungen (Exercises)

Try it yourself

1. Ergänzen Sie den Kasus richtig: Ich mag ___ Film (der Film). Mir gefällt ___ Film (der Film). (Fill in the correct case-marked article for each verb — notice they differ!)
2. Übersetzen Sie beide Sätze auf Deutsch, mit "gefallen" und mit "mögen": "I like the book."
3. Beantworten Sie auf Deutsch: Was findest du langweilig? Was findest du spannend?
4. Fix the mistake: "Ich mag der Kaffee, aber ich finde die Musik nicht gut."
5. Write four sentences about your own opinions — two likes and two dislikes — using at least two different verbs (gefallen, mögen, finden, hassen).
