Kein, keine — saying "no" or "not a"

Grammar rule: kein vs. nicht

German has two negation words that English squashes into one "not." Use kein to negate a noun with ein/eine or no article at all — it behaves exactly like ein/eine, just negative. Use nicht for everything else (verbs, adjectives, nouns that already have der/die/das).

Examples

  • Ist das ein Apfel? Nein, das ist kein Apfel. (Is that an apple? No, that isn't an apple.)
  • Ist das eine Orange? Nein, das ist keine Orange. (Is that an orange? No, it isn't an orange.)
  • Hast du Zeit? Nein, ich habe keine Zeit. (Do you have time? No, I don't have time.) — no article originally, still uses kein
  • Sind das Äpfel? Nein, das sind keine Äpfel. (Are those apples? No, those aren't apples.) — plural

Compare: when "nicht" is correct instead

  • Trinkst du das Wasser? Nein, ich trinke das Wasser nicht. (Are you drinking the water? No, I'm not drinking the water.) — "das Wasser" already has a definite article, so it's "nicht," not "kein"
  • Bist du müde? Nein, ich bin nicht müde. (Are you tired? No, I'm not tired.) — negating an adjective, not a noun

Common mistake: using "nicht ein" instead of "kein"

Literally translating "not a" word-for-word gives "nicht ein" — understandable, but not how German actually negates nouns. German fuses "not" and "a" into the single word kein. Always kein Apfel, never "nicht ein Apfel."

Foods and drinks: usually no article, so usually "kein"

Generic food and drink names are often used with no article at all (Ich trinke Kaffee (I drink coffee)), which means their negation is almost always kein: Ich trinke keinen Kaffee (I don't drink coffee). (You'll notice "kein" gets an extra "-en" ending there — that's the accusative case, covered fully in Chapter 19.)

Exercises

Try it yourself

1. Negate: Das ist ein Brot.
2. Negate: Das ist eine Suppe.
3. Negate: Ich habe Geld (money, no article).
4. Should you use "kein" or "nicht" here: Ich bin ___ krank. (I am not sick.)
5. Should you use "kein" or "nicht" here: Ich trinke ___ Bier. (I don't drink beer — no article originally.)
6. Translate: "Those are not apples."
