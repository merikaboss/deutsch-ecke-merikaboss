Fragepronomen „welch-“ (Übersicht)

Asking "which": overview and the nominative forms

Original topic sheet View "Fragepronomen – Welcher Welche Welches (Overview)" in the Picture Book →

welcher/welche/welches asks "which one" out of a group — narrower than was (Chapter 17), which just asks "what." Like der/die/das (Chapter 10), it changes form to match the gender of the noun that follows it.

Grammar rule: welch- matches der/die/das exactly

ArticleGender/NumberFragepronomenExample
dermasculinewelcherWelcher Pullover ist deiner? (Which sweater is yours?)
diefemininewelcheWelche Tasche gefällt dir? (Which bag do you like?)
dasneuterwelchesWelches Handy hast du? (Which phone do you have?)
diepluralwelcheWelche Schuhe möchtest du? (Which shoes would you like?)

The good news: exactly like Chapter 10's articles, the ending -e stays constant across all four versions — only the article you'd use for that noun tells you which form to pick. If in doubt, ask yourself: "what article would this noun take?" and match it.

Weitere Beispiele

  • Welcher Tag ist heute? (Which day is it today?)
  • Welche Stadt besuchst du? (Which city are you visiting?)
  • Welches Auto ist das? (Which car is that?)
  • Welche Bücher hast du? (Which books do you have?)

Common mistake: using "was für ein" and "welcher" interchangeably

welcher asks which one out of a known, limited set ("which of these sweaters"), while was für ein asks "what kind of" with no set in mind ("what kind of sweater in general"). At this level, just know welcher is your default for "which" — you'll meet was für ein separately later.

Übungen (Exercises)

Try it yourself

1. Ergänzen Sie: ___ Film findest du am besten? (der Film)
2. Ergänzen Sie: ___ Tasche ist deine? (die Tasche)
3. Ergänzen Sie: ___ Buch liest du? (das Buch)
4. Übersetzen Sie: "Which friends are coming?" (die Freunde — plural)
5. Write three "welch-" questions of your own about someone's preferences (movies, food, cities), using each gender form (welcher, welche, welches) at least once.
