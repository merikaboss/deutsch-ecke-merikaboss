💬 Examples using possessives from Chapter 14

  • Meine Mutter heißt Julia. (My mother's name is Julia.)
  • Hast du Geschwister? (Do you have siblings?) — Ja, ich habe einen Bruder und eine Schwester. (Yes, I have a brother and a sister.)
  • Wie viele Kinder hat er? (How many children does he have?)

⚠️ Common mistake: "die Eltern" and "die Geschwister" have no singular use the way you'd expect

die Eltern (parents) and die Geschwister (siblings) are always plural in German, just like in English — there's no "ein Eltern." To talk about one sibling, use der Bruder or die Schwester specifically.

Exercises

✏️ Try it yourself

1. Give the plural of: der Bruder, die Tochter, das Kind.
2. Translate: "My grandmother has two sons and one daughter."
3. Answer in German (about your own family, or an imaginary one): Hast du Geschwister? Wie heißen deine Eltern?
4. Translate: "This is my aunt. That is her husband."
