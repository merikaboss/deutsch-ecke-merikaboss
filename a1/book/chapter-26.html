Untrennbare Verben

Non-separable verbs: prefixes that always stay glued on

Original topic sheet View "Untrennbare Verben" in the Picture Book →

Chapter 21 showed prefixes that split apart. This chapter is the exact opposite: a specific, fixed set of prefixes that never separate from the verb, no matter how the sentence is built.

📐 Grammar rule: these eight prefixes are always non-separable

be-emp-ent-er-
ge-miss-ver-zer-

Unlike separable prefixes (Chapter 21), these are never independent words in their own right — you can't point to a standalone German word "be" or "ver." That's actually a useful test: if the prefix could stand alone as its own word (like an, auf, mit), it's separable; if it can't, it's one of these eight and stays put.

💬 Examples

VerbIn a sentence
besuchen (to visit)Ich besuche meine Oma. (I'm visiting my grandma.)
bezahlen (to pay)Er bezahlt die Rechnung. (He's paying the bill.)
verstehen (to understand)Ich verstehe das Problem nicht. (I don't understand the problem.)
vergessen (to forget)Vergiss die Hausaufgabe nicht! (Don't forget the homework!)
erklären (to explain)Die Lehrerin erklärt die Grammatik. (The teacher explains the grammar.)

⚠️ Common mistake: trying to split "ver-" and friends anyway

Once separable verbs feel natural, there's a temptation to treat every prefixed verb the same way — sending "ver-" to the end too: "Ich stehe das Problem nicht ver." That's wrong; non-separable prefixes stay attached in every tense and every sentence position: always Ich verstehe das Problem nicht.

A preview: why this matters for Chapter 28

This distinction becomes important again in the Perfekt tense — verbs with these eight non-separable prefixes drop the usual ge- in their past participle (besucht, not "gebesucht"), while separable verbs keep ge- in the middle (angekommen). More on that in Chapter 28.

Übungen (Exercises)

✏️ Try it yourself

1. Welches Präfix ist untrennbar: an- oder ver-? Warum?
2. Ergänzen Sie: Ich ___ (besuchen) meine Familie am Wochenende.
3. Fix the mistake: "Ich stehe das nicht ver."
4. Übersetzen Sie: "She forgets the appointment." (vergessen)
5. Sort these verbs into separable or non-separable: anrufen, verstehen, aufstehen, bezahlen, einkaufen, erklären.
