Mein eigenes Testbeispiel

A real evening test from my own A1 studies — with corrections, a full answer key, a template for building your own tests, and where to go after this book

Original topic sheet View my actual handwritten test in the Picture Book →

This last chapter is a little different. It isn't built from a topic sheet but from an actual evening test from my own A1 studies, dated 23.05.2025, written out exactly as it was set: vocabulary, grammar, numbers, questions, reading and writing, in 60 minutes. It covers the absolute core of A1, so if you've worked through the book you should be able to do all of it. Try it under the same conditions, then use the answer key, which explains the tricky items — including a few places where the original test itself was ambiguous. After that, section 9 shows you how to build tests like this for yourself, because writing your own questions is one of the best ways to learn.

7. Notes on the original test

What I'd set differently now

  • The points don't add up to 100. The section totals are 8 + 15 + 7 + 5 + 5 + 10 = 50. Double your score to get a percentage; 30/50 (60 %) is a pass.
  • Part 1 has nine gaps for eight words (task 7 has two), and several gaps accept more than one word: Das ist mein ___ fits Haus, Hund, Bruder, Tisch, Buch, Kugelschreiber. A good vocabulary test gives each gap one clear answer, e.g. Er bellt. Das ist mein ___. The answer key accepts every grammatically correct option.
  • Part 4, question 4 („heute / das Datum / was ist?") leads to „Was ist heute das Datum?", which Germans don't say. The natural questions are Welches Datum ist heute? or Der Wievielte ist heute? (Chapter 44). The prompt above is adjusted.
  • Part 4, question 5 has no verb in the prompt; sein has been added.
  • Part 2 C, question 2: gerne and gern are both correct.

8. Lösungen (Answers)

Parts 1–3

  1. Wortschatz: 1. Haus (also Hund, Bruder, Buch …) · 2. Schule · 3. Freundin · 4. Bruder · 5. Hund (or Tisch) · 6. Buch (or Haus) · 7. Tisch – Kugelschreiber (or Buch) · 8. Kugelschreiber. The key skill is gender: meine needs a feminine noun (Freundin), in die a feminine one (Schule), der a masculine one, das a neuter one, mit einem a masculine or neuter one.
  2. Grammatik A: 1. bin · 2. bist · 3. ist · 4. sind · 5. sind — the formal Sie uses the plural form (Chapter 47).
  3. Grammatik B: 1. Der · 2. Das · 3. Das · 4. Die · 5. Der — das Mädchen is neuter because every -chen noun is.
  4. Grammatik C: 1. b (gehst) · 2. b (spiele) · 3. a (ein) — after Das ist it's Nominativ, so no -en · 4. c (sind) · 5. a (kommst)
  5. Zahlen: siebzehn · vierundzwanzig · einunddreißig · fünfundvierzig · achtundsechzig · neunzig · (ein)hundert. Watch siebzehn (not siebenzehn), dreißig with ß, and sechzig without -s. Units come before tens: vier-und-zwanzig.

Parts 4–6

  1. Fragen: 1. Wo wohnst du? · 2. Wie heißt dein Bruder? · 3. Woher kommst du? · 4. Welches Datum ist heute? / Der Wievielte ist heute? · 5. Wie alt bist du? Question word first, verb second, subject third.
  2. Lesen: 1. Sie heißt Merika. · 2. Sie ist 18 Jahre alt. · 3. Sie kommt aus Uganda. · 4. Sie hat zwei Geschwister (einen Bruder und eine Schwester). · 5. Englisch und Deutsch. die Geschwister (siblings) is a plural-only word.
  3. Schreiben — model answer: Ich heiße Grace. Ich bin 20 Jahre alt. Ich komme aus Kenia und wohne jetzt in Nairobi. Ich habe zwei Brüder, aber keine Schwester. In meiner Freizeit lese ich gern und spiele Volleyball.
    Marking (2 points per sentence): 1 point for the content, 1 point for a correct sentence — verb in position 2, correct ein/kein ending, nouns capitalised.

9. Build your own evening test

Making a test forces you to know the answer precisely — you can't write a good gap for einen unless you know why it's einen. Once a week, take a chapter you've finished and build a small test with this template. Swap tests with a study partner if you can.

A 50-point template

PartTask typePointsTips for writing it
1. Wortschatzgap fill with a word bank8one gap per sentence, one correct word — use context clues (Er bellt.)
2. Grammatikforms, articles, multiple choice15make the wrong options realistic learner mistakes
3. Zahlen / Uhrzeit / Datumwrite out in words7include the traps: 17, 30, 60, 1st, 3rd, 7th
4. Fragenbuild questions5give the words in the wrong order, not just the question word
5. Lesenshort text + questions or richtig/falsch5add one distractor (a second date, an old address)
6. Schreibenshort message or self-introduction10give 3–5 content points and mark content + correctness separately

Write the answer key before you take the test a week later. If you find a gap with two possible answers, rewrite it — exactly the kind of fix made in section 7.

10. Where to go from here

Das war's — geschafft! (That's it — done!)

If you've worked from Chapter 0 to here, you've covered the ground of a complete A1 course: articles, pronouns and possessives in all four cases, present, Perfekt and the Präteritum of sein and haben, modal and separable verbs, questions, numbers, times and dates, and the real situations — shopping, the doctor, the bank, the post office, flats, trains, forms and letters. That's genuinely a lot. Du hast das geschafft! (You did it!)

  • Consolidate: redo Chapters 50–53 in two weeks without looking at your old answers. Revise every chapter where you lost points.
  • Speak: practise the three parts of the speaking exam out loud (Chapter 0 describes the format) — record yourself introducing yourself, asking topic questions and making requests.
  • Listen and read every day: short, slow German — learner podcasts, children's news, public signs, menus, timetables. Ten minutes a day beats two hours on Sunday.
  • Take the exam: if you want the certificate, look for official Start Deutsch 1 practice material and exam dates from the Goethe-Institut or telc, and do one complete model test under time pressure first.
  • Keep this book open: use the Picture Book and these chapters as references while you move on to A2 material. When A2 material is ready, it will appear on the hub page.
  • Ask: the WhatsApp group is the place for questions a book can't answer — don't be shy.

A reminder: I'm a learner sharing my own study journey, not a certified teacher. I've checked everything carefully, but if something here disagrees with your teacher or with official exam material, trust them — and let me know, so I can fix it.
