Demonstrativartikel „dies-“

"This/these": pointing at something specific

Original topic sheet View "Demonstrativartikel – Dies Dieser Diese Dieses" in the Picture Book →

dieser/diese/dieses (this) replaces der/die/das when you want to point at something specific, rather than just refer to it generally. Same gender-tracking rule as always, and — you'll notice a pattern by now — the same ending logic as Chapter 10's articles and Chapter 35's welch-.

Beispiele

  • Dieser Mann ist müde. (This man is tired.) — nominative
  • Ich sehe diesen Mann. (I see this man.) — accusative
  • Ich helfe diesem Mann. (I help this man.) — dative
  • Diese Bücher sind neu. (These books are new.) — plural

There's also an invariable short form, dies, used the way English uses "this" as a standalone pronoun: Was ist dies? (What is this?) — but dieser/diese/dieses in front of a noun is far more common in everyday speech.

Common mistake: forgetting "dies-" changes exactly like an article

Some learners freeze dieser as one word for every situation, since English "this" never changes. But dies- is not optional grammar — it fully agrees with the noun's gender and case, just like der/die/das: Ich kenne diese Kinder, not "Ich kenne dieser Kinder."

Übungen (Exercises)

Try it yourself

1. Ergänzen Sie: ___ Frau ist meine Lehrerin. (die Frau — Nominativ)
2. Ergänzen Sie: Ich kaufe ___ Äpfel. (die Äpfel — Akkusativ, Plural)
3. Ergänzen Sie: Ich spreche mit ___ Kindern. (die Kinder — Dativ, Plural)
4. Übersetzen Sie: "This book is interesting, but this film is boring." (use two different genders of dies-)
5. Fix the mistake: "Ich sehe dieser Mann."
6. Point at (describe) three objects around you right now, in German, using dieser/diese/dieses correctly for each one's gender.
