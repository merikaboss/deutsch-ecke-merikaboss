„Sie“ im Deutschen

One small word, four different jobs — a review chapter

Original topic sheet View "Sie in German – All Meanings and Uses" in the Picture Book →

You've actually met every meaning of sie already, scattered across earlier chapters. This is a deliberate review chapter that pulls all four together in one place, since confusing them is extremely common even at higher levels.

📐 The four meanings of sie/Sie

MeaningCapitalized?Where you met itExample
you (formal)Always: SieChapter 6Können Sie mir helfen?
theyNo: sieChapter 6Sie lernen Deutsch.
sheNo: sieChapter 6Sie heißt Anna.
herself (reflexive)No: sich (not sie!)Chapter 42Sie wäscht sich.

Note the fourth row carefully: the reflexive pronoun itself is actually sich, not sie — the subject sie (she) triggers the reflexive pronoun sich later in the sentence. They look related but are grammatically two different words doing two different jobs.

📐 How to tell them apart in a real sentence

  • Talking to someone politely → Sie (formal you)
  • Talking about two or more people → sie (they)
  • Talking about one woman → sie (she)
  • The action bounces back onto the woman herself → sich (herself)

💬 All four, side by side

  • Entschuldigen Sie bitte. (Excuse me. — formal you)
  • Sie wohnen in Berlin. (They live in Berlin. — they)
  • Sie ist meine Schwester. (She is my sister. — she)
  • Sie freut sich über das Geschenk. (She is happy about the gift. — she + herself)

⚠️ Common mistake: guessing wrong when unsure

The topic sheet's own advice is worth repeating: when in doubt about whether to use du or Sie with someone (Chapter 6), default to the capitalized formal Sie — it's always safe and polite, even if slightly too formal, whereas guessing du with a stranger can genuinely come across as rude.

Übungen (Exercises)

✏️ Try it yourself

1. Welche Bedeutung hat "sie" hier: "Sie kommen aus Frankreich." (you formal, they, or she?)
2. Welche Bedeutung hat "Sie" hier: "Wie heißen Sie?"
3. Übersetzen Sie und markieren Sie, welche Bedeutung von "sie/Sie" Sie benutzt haben: "She combs her hair. They play football. Can you (formal) help me?"
4. Fix the mistake: "Sie wäscht sie." when the intended meaning is "She washes herself."
5. Write four short sentences, one for each meaning of sie/Sie, clearly labeling which meaning each one uses.
