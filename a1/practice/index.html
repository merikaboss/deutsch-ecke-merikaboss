Übungsbuch

The practice book — Lesen, Schreiben, Sprechen

The Picture Book shows a topic and the Written Book explains it. This is where you use it. Each part is read as turnable pages, just like the Written Book, and every unit ends with tasks that mark themselves and tell you why an answer was wrong.

Teil 1 · Lesen

Reading

Stories to read right through, and the short everyday German you have to understand fast — signs, opening hours, notes, emails, small ads.

Zum Lesen → Teil 2 · Schreiben

Writing

Forms, sentence building, text messages, notes, and informal and formal emails — how each one is built, then writing your own with a model text to compare against.

Zum Schreiben → Teil 3 · Sprechen

Speaking

Introducing yourself, spelling and numbers, questions from word cards, requests and everyday conversations. Answer out loud, record yourself and compare with the model.

So arbeiten Sie mit dem Übungsbuch

How every unit works, in all three parts.

1 · Lernen

Each unit opens with what you need: the text type, the phrases, the grammar that matters for it.

2 · Üben

Work through the tasks on the pages. Type into the fields, pick an answer, or say it out loud.

3 · Prüfen

One button at the end marks everything, explains the mistakes and shows model answers.

4 · Wiederholen

Your result is saved on this device and shown in the list, so you can see what to do again.
