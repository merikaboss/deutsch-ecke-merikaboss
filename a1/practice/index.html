Übungsbuch

The practice book — where you use it, not just read it

The Picture Book shows you the topic and the Written Book explains it. This is the part where you find out whether it actually went in. Every task here marks itself the moment you press the button, and tells you why an answer was wrong — not just that it was.

The German in the reading texts is left clean, with no translations in brackets. That is deliberate: a translation sitting next to the word is no longer reading practice. The vocabulary list and the full English version are one tap away underneath instead.

In Arbeit

A note on what this is

These are my own practice materials, written from scratch while I was working through A1 myself. They follow the shape of the tasks you meet in real German courses, but nothing here is copied from anybody's exam papers, and this is not exam preparation — it is just the practice half of my own notes, shared.

Hören (listening) is not here yet. I would rather leave it out than put up something half-built, so it comes later.
